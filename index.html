The problem

Too many CX deployments underdeliver. The platform is rarely the reason.

Modern CX platforms are ready for AI-first customer experience. Legacy deployment models are not.

BIG CONSULTING FIRMS

Sold by partners, staffed by juniors

The team winning the work is rarely the team doing it — and every gap between pitch and reality arrives as a change order.

TYPICAL RESELLERS

Configured, not engineered

A phone system can be configured. An AI agent must be designed, tested, and tuned before launch — retuned once live customers arrive.

DIY DEPLOYMENTS

Stalled by gaps no one was staffed to close

Capable internal teams hit walls on integrations, data quality, and edge cases falling outside anyone's day job.

TECHNOLOGY FIRST

Automation layered over a broken process

Deploying before the journey is redesigned makes the wrong experience faster, not better.

PILOT PURGATORY

Proofs of concept never reaching scale

A contained pilot proves the technology works. It rarely survives production volume, live integrations, and compliance review.

VANITY METRICS

Deflection mistaken for resolution

Containment rates look strong while customers repeat themselves, escalate, or leave. Resolution is the number worth measuring.

KNOWLEDGE DEBT

Agents grounded in stale content

An AI agent is only as accurate as the knowledge behind it — and few content estates were built to be read by a machine.

INTEGRATION REALITY

Demos not surviving production

A demo answers questions. Production must take action inside CRM, ERP, and ticketing systems — where timelines quietly slip.

LAUNCH AS FINISH LINE

Go-live treated as the end of the project

Products change, policies change, and customers find new ways to phrase old questions. AI performance drifts. Systems left alone degrade.

AI is not a set-it-and-forget-it technology. It requires a different kind of partner.

How we deliver

Your deployment is software engineering. We run it with the same discipline.

Every engagement follows a consistent five-phase model — scoped to your environment, whether the work involves a contact center migration, enterprise voice, or an AI agent build.

Design decisions get made on working prototypes, not diagrams. You see the solution before we build it.

01 SETUP

Setup

Team alignment, workspace provisioning, licensing, and agreement on project objectives.

02 DESIGN

Design

Workshops and process discovery, integration architecture, and concept flows reviewed with your team.

03 BUILD

Build

Configuration, integrations, and administrator training, with builds validated as we go.

04 DEPLOY

Deploy

User acceptance testing, live training, a go/no-go decision, and dedicated support through cutover.

05 CLOSE

Close

Handover, roadmap checkpoints, and a project review against the objectives set in phase one.
